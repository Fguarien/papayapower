PapayaPower — installation

1. Drag PapayaPower into the Applications folder.
2. Open it. macOS says it cannot check the developer (the app is not notarised):
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway".
3. In PapayaPower > Settings > General, tick "Open at login".
   Leave the role on "Member": the family master computer will see this Mac.
